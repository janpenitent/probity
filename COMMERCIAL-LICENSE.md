## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL's key obligation: if you run a modified version of Probity as a
network-accessible service, you must offer that service's users the complete
corresponding source under the AGPL. You likely need a separate commercial
license if you want to:

- Embed Probity in a **closed-source** product or distribute it without
  releasing your own source.
- Offer Probity (modified or not) as part of a **SaaS / hosted service**
  without making your source available to your users.
- Combine Probity with proprietary code in a way the AGPL's copyleft would
  otherwise reach.
- Obtain a warranty, indemnification, or a support agreement.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's source-disclosure and copyleft
obligations under negotiated terms, and is the same agreement under which the
proprietary **Probity Enterprise** overlay (live cloud connectors, the
scheduler/dashboard service layer, audit PDF, and DORA / EU AI Act
multi-framework mapping) is sold.
